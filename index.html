Stock Board

Stocks that are moving, heavily traded and talked about, from the latest snapshot (taken every 15 minutes on business days, pre-market through after-hours). Gainers are the biggest gains, Most Active the heaviest trading, Buzz the most Reddit mentions. The All tab only lists stocks that are on at least 2 of the 3 rankings. Sort by Newest (stocks that started trending most recently, shown as “35m ago” on each tile) or by Ranking. Each tile has a short AI summary of why the stock is trending, rewritten on every update from recent headlines. Star a stock to keep it handy. Small and penny stocks are filtered out.
